01 — TWO METRICS

Being Googled and being recommended are two different games

Each brand is measured two ways. Right = more people Google it each month. Up = AI engines named it on more of our 16 questions.

The two don't move together. ServiceMax gets 4,010 searches a month — eight times Neuron7 — but most of that is its own customers logging in. SupportLogic gets searched more than most vendors here and was never recommended once. And Neuron7 sits second among the AI-native vendors on both counts, behind Aquant.

THE GEMINI GAP

The engine split is not even. Perplexity names Neuron7 on 7 of 16 questions — the most of any engine. Gemini names it once, while naming Aquant on 11 and ServiceMax on 10. A buyer who asks Google's engine gets a shortlist Neuron7 is essentially not on.

03 — WHAT AI READS

Engines read pages, then recommend whoever those pages talk about

Every answer came with its sources and we captured all of them: 183 domains across the 64 answers. Whoever wrote the pages writes the shortlist — and near the top of the reading list is TrueContext, an FSM vendor whose "best AI field service platforms" roundup ranks itself first. neuron7.ai is cited in 7 of 64 answers — but Aquant's two sites together reach 10.

THE SHORT VERSION

truecontext.com is cited in 9 of 64 answers — second only to Salesforce's own site. TrueContext is a competitor, and its roundup is one of the pages deciding who makes the AI shortlist. The listicle lists Neuron7 fifth. That single page is doing a measurable share of Neuron7's category marketing.

THE EMPTY SHELF

On "how do we capture expert technicians' knowledge before they retire" — Neuron7's flagship outcome — no engine named any vendor at all. All four answered with process advice. Nobody owns that question yet. The same is nearly true of mean-time-to-resolution and first-time-fix: one vendor mention each across eight answers.

WHO TOOK THE FAILURE-PREDICTION QUESTION

"Software that predicts equipment failures" went to a different category entirely: Augury, IBM Maximo and Siemens Senseye — sensor-based predictive-maintenance vendors — in three of four engines each. Engines read "predict failures" as an IoT problem, not a service-intelligence one.

The use-case questions aren't lost to a competitor. Nobody has claimed them — Aquant appears in 2 of those 20 answers, Neuron7 in 0.

When the buyer describes the problem

The five use-case questions are, almost word for word, the outcomes on neuron7.ai: retain expert knowledge, resolve issues faster, prevent downtime, ramp new technicians. Zero mentions in 20 answers. A buyer who starts from the pain never hears the name.

The contradiction engines don't notice

Asked to compare Neuron7 and Aquant by name, Gemini's verdict is: "Choose Neuron7 if your primary bottleneck is complex troubleshooting, low first-time fix rates and long onboarding cycles." Ask the same engine "what tools improve first-time fix rate" without a name, and Neuron7 is not in the answer. The engines know what Neuron7 is for. They just never retrieve it from the problem side — because the pages they read on those questions don't mention it.

Who gets named instead: first-time fix goes to Salesforce and SightCall; new-technician ramp to Microsoft and Salesforce; failure prediction to Augury, IBM Maximo and Siemens Senseye. Knowledge capture goes to nobody.

The 3-hours-to-3-seconds and 46%-faster numbers engines quote all trace back to neuron7.ai itself. Independent, third-party versions of those proofs don't exist yet for engines to find.

07 — METHODOLOGY

Exactly what we did

SHARE OF MODEL

21 prompts × 4 engines = 84 answers

16 category questions (counted) + 5 questions naming Neuron7 (read separately), each put to all four engines. Every engine ran its current production model with live web search on, so every answer is sourced and the sources were captured. No counted question names Neuron7. One pass per engine; all 84 answers returned.

SCORING

Mention tally

A brand scores once per answer however often it is named, so this counts how widely it is recommended, not how much ink it gets. A question's own subject does not score (Aquant in "alternatives to Aquant", Salesforce in the Salesforce question). The brand list was built from the answers, not written in advance. Citation URLs are stripped first, so a vendor that only appears in a link does not score. 279 mentions, 36 vendors, 64 answers.

SHARE OF SEARCH

Branded search demand

Branded Google volume (US, 12-month average) across 8 brands × 15 modifiers, with spelling variants that Google counts as the same query removed before summing. Ambiguous brand words modelled from their unambiguous queries and reported as a range. Growth read from the month-by-month series, not the average.
