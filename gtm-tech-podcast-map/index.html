Where GTM tech actually advertises

Look at where go-to-market software buys podcast ads and a surprise jumps out: the funded players barely touch B2B or sales podcasts. They buy news, culture, sports, and tech shows — reaching the operator as a person, at consumer CPMs.

The finding

GTM advertising splits in two: a mass-reach play (buy mainstream shows for scale) and an endemic play (buy the one niche your buyer already lives in). The best-funded brands almost all pick reach.

Playbook 1 — mass reach

Skip the B2B ghetto. Buy the news / culture / sports / tech shows your buyer listens to off the clock. Bigger audiences, cheaper CPMs, premium halo.
