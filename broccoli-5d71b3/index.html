The scoreboard

Google Search

9 text ads all-time under Broccoli's legal entity Martian Mobility Inc., first seen March 28. Seven went dark by late April; two brand ads are still live today. With no conversion tag on the site, Google's bidding has never received a single conversion signal from them.

Conversion tracking

We inspected broccoli.com's tag stack: no Google Ads tag, no Tag Manager, no Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok, Reddit, X or Bing pixels. Only Segment. No paid channel can measure a conversion or build a retargeting audience on this site.

Absent

"hvac call answering software", "ai answering service hvac", "ai receptionist home services": Broccoli appears on none of them, paid or organic. Sameday, Smith.ai, Dialzara, Retell and a wall of comparison posts fill every result.

The finding

The spend exists. The measurement doesn't.

Most companies build the tracking and never turn on the spend. Broccoli did the opposite: live Google ads since March, and not one conversion tag anywhere on the site to tell those ads what worked. Every click is bought blind — no smart bidding signal, no retargeting audience, no way for the incoming marketing team to know what the last six months of spend produced.

Where we'd start

01

Method

Honest hedges

Ad-library databases are samples and undercount — we treat "zero" as "nothing detectable" and triangulated every Broccoli reading across the Transparency Center, live creatives and the site's own tag stack.
