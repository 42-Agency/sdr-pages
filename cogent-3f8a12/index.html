The finding

The conquest motion is proven. It's one keyword wide, and it's flying blind.

Someone at Cogent already built the hard part: a dedicated "Astelia Alternative" landing page and a live conquest campaign against its closest AI-native rival. But that's the entire program: no conversion tag measures it, the incumbent auctions ("tenable alternative" runs a $157 CPC) go uncontested, and Tenable is running the same conquest play at 1,000-creative scale, against Astelia and BreachRx both. The playbook works. Nobody is in the seat to scale it.

Where we'd start

Three moves, in order

01

Tag before you scale

The live Astelia campaign runs with no Google Ads conversion tag: spend with no scoreboard. Installing it is a same-day fix inside the GTM container that already holds the Meta, LinkedIn and Reddit pixels. Measure first, then pour.

02

Widen the conquest to the incumbents

The /resources/astelia page proves the pattern; /resources/tenable and /resources/qualys don't exist yet. Build them, then enter the "alternative" and pricing auctions where displacement deals actually start, and claim "autonomous vulnerability remediation" before SentinelOne and Vicarius finish taking the category you named.

03

Light up the pixels you already have

Meta and Reddit pixels have been collecting site audiences with no campaigns behind them. Retargeting warm traffic (off the back of a $42M Series A news cycle) is the cheapest pipeline in paid. The setup cost is already paid.
