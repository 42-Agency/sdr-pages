The finding

The measurement lives on the wrong channels.

Google and Bing are instrumented properly, and they funnel into a site with exactly one page. LinkedIn, where Bobyard runs more ads than both active competitors combined, has zero instrumentation: every dollar spent there produces no retargeting audience, no lookalike seed, and no conversion data. Whoever fills the open Demand Gen Manager and GTM Engineer seats inherits a channel mix where the spend and the measurement don't overlap.

Where we'd start

Three moves, in order

01

Install the Insight Tag today

It's a ten-minute change in the GTM container already on the site. From that moment, 429 ads' worth of LinkedIn traffic starts building retargeting audiences, matched audiences and conversion data: the raw material your incoming Demand Gen Manager needs on day one.

02

Give the clicks somewhere to land

Every paid click on every channel currently lands on the homepage, because every bobyard.com URL resolves there. Method

Honest hedges

Ad-library databases are samples and undercount: we treat "zero" as "nothing detectable." LinkedIn's library keeps ads about a year and counts creative variants, so 429 is a 12-month footprint, not concurrent campaigns. Bobyard may capture partial conversion data through UTMs into GA4 and HubSpot; the verified gap is specifically the platform-side one: no Insight Tag means no retargeting, no matched audiences, no LinkedIn conversion optimization. Bing has no public ad library, so pixel presence is the best available signal there.
