Key Finding

Tulip has the best message in this group. The problem is where it's running. The "start with one use case, compliance built in, no 18-month rollout" story shows up on LinkedIn and almost nowhere else. There are 40 Tulip ads on Google with none of it, all under one person's personal ad account. And nobody in the category is on Meta except MaintainX, who has it to themselves.

The positioning is genuinely different from everyone else here, so that's not the issue. The issue is that the best story is stuck on one channel, the biggest channel says nothing, and a whole platform is being handed to a competitor. None of that takes more budget to fix.

Four Gaps Worth Taking

01 · NOBODY'S ON META BUT MAINTAINX

Tulip, Parsable, and Poka run zero ads on Meta. MaintainX is the only one there, and they're going hard. The people who'd actually champion Tulip inside an account (the ops and frontline managers) live on their phones, not on LinkedIn. Right now MaintainX is the only one reaching them.

02 · 40 GOOGLE ADS THAT SAY NOTHING

Google is Tulip's biggest channel by ad count, and the ads there barely say anything. They're thin, and they all run under one person's personal ad account. The sharp story on LinkedIn never made it to Google, which is exactly where people search when they're ready to buy. That's a mismatch you fix with creative, not dollars.

03 · THE PROOF IS WHISPERED

Poka opens with big customer names. MaintainX opens with dollar figures. Tulip has both (VEKA cut quality escapes 88%, TICO built a full MES from one checklist) but those numbers sit three lines into the copy instead of being the headline. The good stuff is there. It's just whispered.

04 · "AI FOR REGULATED OPS" IS WIDE OPEN

MaintainX is all-in on AI, but for maintenance. No one is putting AI and compliance together for regulated manufacturing. Tulip is the only one who actually could, and they're not saying it in their ads yet.

That's what we can see from the outside. The rest is a conversation.
