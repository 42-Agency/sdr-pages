The finding

Demand creation without demand capture.

245 LinkedIn ads are warming up operations and finance leaders every day. When those buyers do what warmed-up buyers do (google the use case, google "duvo ai pricing", google the alternatives), Duvo isn't there. The search layer that should harvest LinkedIn's work has decayed to 2 text ads, and the conversion tag that would measure it sits installed on the site with almost nothing feeding it.

That window is open, not permanent.

Where we'd start

Three moves, in order

01

Own the use-case auctions

"freight audit software" is 210 searches/mo at $38.72: small, high-intent, and the exact story Duvo's homepage already tells with the XPO invoice walkthrough. A dedicated freight-audit landing page plus a focused campaign puts Duvo in front of buyers Trimble and Loop currently get by default.

02

Put a floor under the brand SERP

Nobody bids on "duvo ai" (including Duvo), and Capterra's pricing-and-alternatives page sits at #5. Owning the brand auction is cheap insurance, and a proper pricing/alternatives page takes the narrative back from the directory.

03

Restart the harvest layer under LinkedIn

The Google Ads tag, HubSpot, Clarity and GA4 are all live on duvo.ai. Retargeting the traffic 245 LinkedIn ads generate, plus competitor pricing and alternative terms at seed-appropriate budgets, is the cheapest pipeline available: the instrumentation cost is already paid.

Receipts

How we checked, and where we'd hedge

Method

12 live Google SERP pulls (US, desktop + mobile) across brand, competitor and use-case terms · paid-keyword profiles for all four domains via DataForSEO Labs · full-format creative counts and last-seen dates from the Google Ads Transparency Center · LinkedIn and Meta ad libraries · direct inspection of duvo.ai's tag stack, including the GTM container.

Honest hedges

Ad-library databases are samples and undercount, so we treat "zero" as "nothing detectable" and triangulated Duvo's Google reading across the Transparency Center, the Labs keyword database and the site's tag stack. LinkedIn's library doesn't expose per-ad dates, so 245 is the library total, not a count of simultaneously live ads. "duvo" brand search volume is contaminated by unrelated brands (Fujifilm's Duvo lenses, a pet-products line), so we quote no brand-volume number.
