Why Gemini never names us

Gemini answers from listicles

Its most-cited sources for these questions are "best agency" roundup pages — and the top three are owned by competitors: directiveconsulting.com, understoryagency.com, piperocket.digital. If you're not in the roundups, you don't exist to Gemini.

Claude searches wider

Claude pulls from a broader set of pages per answer and leans on what it already knows about each firm — which is why it can describe us accurately and slot us into the right buyer stage without a listicle telling it to.

What actually moves this number

  1. Get into third-party "best agency" roundups. Digital PR to the pages Gemini already cites. This is the single fix that flips Gemini from 0/16.
  2. Publish the page that answers "who combines demand gen + RevOps." It's our positioning and we lose that exact question today.
  3. Build the review footprint. Claude flags it unprompted; a handful of verified G2/Clutch reviews closes the only negative in the brand answers.

Method + what's next

What we ran

16 buyer questions (discovery, channel, use-case, switching) + 4 questions about 42 directly, on Sep 5, 2026. Engines: Claude (Opus, with live web search) and Gemini (2.5 Flash, with Google Search grounding). Every answer saved verbatim.

Two engines, not four

ChatGPT and Perplexity are in the standard method but were blocked this run (data-provider access expired). They get backfilled next wave — expect ChatGPT to look more like Claude and Perplexity more like Gemini, since Perplexity also leans on listicles.

This is Wave 1

The same 20 questions get re-run quarterly, unchanged. Individual answers vary run to run; the pattern is what we track. The number to move by Wave 2: Gemini above zero.
