The finding

The fundraise created the demand. Competitors are catching it.

The $30M Series A did its job: roughly 2,400 people a month now search "runlayer", at a $6.50 cost per click, the cheapest, highest-intent traffic Runlayer will ever get. But there's no pricing page for them to land on, no ads above the results, and the pages answering their questions belong to TrueFoundry, MintMCP and Latio. In April, Andrew wrote that product is ahead of marketing. On this evidence, the gap has compounded.
