The Two Axes Evaluators Use

When a CISO or DPO shortlists DSPM, two questions split the field. LightBeam's quadrant matters more than its feature list.

1 Identity-centric vs Data-centric

Some platforms anchor on who can access what (identity). Others anchor on what data exists and where (data discovery). LightBeam straddles both, which is a feature in deals where the buyer wants one platform, and a liability in deals where the buyer's already standardized on identity (Sailpoint, Saviynt) or pure data discovery (BigID).

Identity-leaning
Symmetry, Cyera (drift), Normalyze
Data-leaning
BigID, Varonis, Securiti, OneTrust

2 Privacy-stack vs Security-stack budget

The other split is who owns the budget. Privacy-stack DSPM (OneTrust, Securiti, Ethyca) rides DPO and legal lines. Security-stack DSPM (Varonis, Cyera, Normalyze) rides CISO and SOC lines. LightBeam shows up in both shortlists, which means two different sales motions and two different content libraries.

DPO/Privacy budget
OneTrust, Securiti, Ethyca, BigID
CISO/Security budget
Varonis, Cyera, Normalyze, Symmetry

Where LightBeam Lands

WIN
Mid-market financial services + tech
Where the buyer wants one vendor for PII discovery, DSAR, and access governance. Snap Finance, Veridian, AGA Benefits, Invisible Tech in the public references match this shape.
WIN
Net-new buyers who haven't standardized on OneTrust
OneTrust's GRC-platform breadth becomes a switching cost in renewal deals. Net-new buyers without that lock-in are the cleanest LightBeam wins.
CONTESTED
CISO-led security-budget deals
Cyera and Normalyze have raised more, hire more, and run more paid against pure CISO buyers. LightBeam wins these on integrated DSAR + access angles, loses them on pure cloud-data-detection benchmarks.
LOSE
Pure cloud-data-detection RFPs against Cyera
When the eval criteria collapse to "scan our AWS+Snowflake fastest," the field narrows to Cyera, Normalyze, Sentra. Don't fight there — re-frame the eval.

On the Pavilion ask

You said you're shifting to digital + PR + intent-led, and that ICP and messaging are dialed. The lever isn't more channels. It's keying spend to evaluator intent so the call lands when the buyer is actually in market.

Three signal layers worth wiring

1
Category-page intent. G2 / Gartner Peer Insights / TrustRadius visits to the DSPM category and to direct competitors. Highest-signal accounts. 24-hour response window.
2
Hiring intent. New CISO/DPO postings, plus net-new privacy team builds. 60-90 day buying windows. The job posting itself is a content brief.
3
Regulatory intent. State privacy law triggers (Texas TDPSA, Oregon, Delaware), SEC cyber disclosure timelines, EU AI Act phased dates. Each one creates a 30-90 day eval cohort.
