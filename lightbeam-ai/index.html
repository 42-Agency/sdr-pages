The play this data implies

Not a proposal. Just what the CI signals would tell anyone running this category.

1
Concentrated paid against the BigID-shaped Google gap
High-intent non-brand DSPM keywords + competitor displacement (BigID, OneTrust). The lane is open today and won't be in 90 days.
2
LinkedIn always-on at frequency, not pulses
Cyera gets 90% of their air cover from LinkedIn alone. ~30 impressions per ICP contact per 30-day flight is the threshold that flips recall. Frequency target, not lead-volume target.
3
Triple down on analyst air cover into eval windows
Gartner TRiSM 2026 + four Hype Cycle 2025 placements + IDC Major Player are sitting underused. BigID is paying to amplify Forrester. The asset is already paid for; the amplification is the gap.
4
Demo volume as the north-star, not lead count
If "right point in the buyer's journey" is the problem, lead count is the wrong metric. Demos held by ICP-fit account is the only number that proves the timing fix worked.
