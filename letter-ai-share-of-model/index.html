05 — HOLISTIC SHARE OF SEARCH

Branded search, measured properly

Branded Google demand across the five-vendor set (US, 12-month average). Two of the names are common words — "letter ai" collides with AI cover-letter generators, "seismic" with earthquakes — so a naive single-term pull lies in both directions. Following Kantar's holistic method (full long-tail + ambiguity modeling) puts Letter AI's real branded demand at ~1,900/month, ~6% of the set — the smallest, roughly 10× below Highspot. That is exactly what you'd expect of a company four months past its Series B, and it's why the search bar is the wrong scoreboard for Letter AI right now.

07 — WHEN LETTER AI IS NAMED

The problem is discovery, not reputation

We also asked both engines directly about Letter AI ("is Letter AI good?", "Letter AI vs Highspot", "Letter AI vs Seismic", "companies like Letter AI"). When prompted by name, the models are accurate and favorable. The knowledge exists — it just isn't triggered by category questions.

STRONG

The model already knows the product

ChatGPT rates Letter AI 9/10, cites its 4.9 G2 score, and calls it a "strong contender… worth including on a shortlist alongside Highspot and Seismic." It accurately describes Letter Compass, AI roleplay, Deal Pursuit / AI Sales Rooms, RFP automation, and the 2026 funding. This is real, specific product knowledge.

THE GEO LEVER

"AI-native" is the credited difference

Both engines frame Letter AI as built around AI from day one rather than "bolting AI onto a legacy CMS" — the exact positioning. When asked head-to-head, they give it the AI-first edge over Highspot and Seismic. The job is getting that framing into the unprompted category and "alternatives to" answers.

THE CEILING

Filed as "emerging," not default

When it does appear, Letter AI lands in the "AI-first challenger" tier (with GTM Buddy, Luster, Yoodli), not the enterprise shortlist. That's a positioning-and-proof job: customer logos (Lenovo, Adobe, Roku), analyst placement, and third-party lists move it from emerging to default.

08 — WHAT TO DO

Four moves off this data

01

Get onto the third-party lists both engines read

Perplexity names whoever G2, SelectHub, fitgap and Gartner rank; ChatGPT leans on the same sources. Letter AI is absent from the "best sales enablement software" and "best AI-native enablement" listicles. Earning placement there is the fastest, most measurable GEO lever — a surface you can own, not a brand-awareness spend.

02

Own the "AI-native enablement" lane

The one query built for exactly what Letter AI sells named Spekit, Mutiny and 1mind — not Letter AI. Concentrate owned and earned content on "AI-native / AI-first revenue enablement" so the models file Letter AI at the front of the category it's defining, before an incumbent's AI retrofit takes the label.

03

Publish the comparison & switching content

The "alternatives to Highspot / Seismic" answers list Mindtickle, Showpad, Allego by default. Letter AI already wins the head-to-head when asked directly — turn that into indexed "Letter AI vs Highspot / vs Seismic" and migration pages so it gets named in the unprompted switching queries too.

04

Track Share of Model, not branded search

For an AI-native challenger, the AI answer layer is the discovery surface — and the first place a $40M brand push should register. Lock this run as Baseline Wave 1 and re-run the identical prompt set quarterly, per engine and per cluster, to see the number move before it shows up in pipeline.

09 — METHODOLOGY

Exactly what we did

Three data pulls, run 2026-07-23, fully repeatable. Built to the Kantar "holistic" standard — full prompt/keyword spectrum and ambiguity correction — rather than a naive single-term read.

SHARE OF GEO

20 prompts × 2 engines

16 category prompts (counted toward share) + 4 brand-defensive / comparison prompts, each run live on ChatGPT and Perplexity via BrightData LLM scrapers (web_data_chatgpt_ai_insights, web_data_perplexity_ai_insights). Grok attempted; scraper timed out.

SCORING

Mention tally

A brand scores when named in an answer. A query's own subject (Highspot / Seismic in "alternatives to…") is excluded from its tally. 182 mentions across 32 category answers; share = mentions ÷ total. Prompts grouped into 6 journey clusters for the coverage map.

SEARCH + TRENDS

Holistic, via DataForSEO

Branded Google volume (US, 12-mo avg) on a brand × long-tail matrix; ambiguous tokens ("letter ai", "seismic") modeled by attribution from disambiguated siblings, reported as a floor→modeled range. Google Trends: 52 weekly points, US, normalized 0–100.

Caveats

Two engines is a directional read, not a census — but the finding here is categorical (0 of 32), not a close decimal. LLM answers vary run-to-run; a zero result is the most stable kind. Branded-search disambiguation still slightly understates the most ambiguous names. First-party Google Search Console was not available (external analysis) — it's the natural fourth surface to add. Single snapshot — re-run quarterly.
