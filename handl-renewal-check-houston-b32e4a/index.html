Fair questions

What everyone asks first

Are you a broker?
No. Handl is not a broker, TPA, or carrier. We work alongside your broker, not around them. Many brokers use this same data with their clients; yours can too.
Is a cheaper imaging center as good?
For a standard MRI or CT, independent imaging centers are accredited, run the same classes of equipment, and have scans read by board-certified radiologists. A price gap this wide comes from how the contract was written, not from the quality of the scan. Anyone who needs hospital-based imaging should still have it there.
Is any of this PHI?
No. The check starts from zip codes only. No names, no claims, no health information of any kind.
We're mid-cycle. Is it too late?
No. Nothing here touches your renewal itself, so the check is useful at any point in the cycle. Before the quote lands is just when it helps most.
Do we have to change carriers or networks?
No. The check is information about the plan you already have. What you do with it is your call.
