The finding

LinkedIn builds the audience. Google harvests it. Amperos only pays for the first half.

209 LinkedIn creatives are teaching RCM leaders that an AI worker can clear their denial backlog. When those same people later search the category — "denial management software", "ai medical billing" — they land on Waystar and Rivet organically, or on Candid Health, the only competitor running Google ads. Amperos wired up the conversion tag and never launched a campaign behind it.

Where we'd start

Three moves, in order

01

Turn on the tag you already installed

Launch search on the category terms — "denial management software", "claim denial management", "ai medical billing", "revenue cycle management software". The conversion tag is already wired into GTM; the measurement setup cost is paid. Only Candid is in these auctions.

02

Give the LinkedIn spend a capture layer

209 creatives are creating demand that currently exits through Waystar's and Rivet's organic results. Pair the LinkedIn program with search coverage and a comparison page against the legacy denial-management tools that own the SERP, so the demand you create comes back to you.

03

Build air cover before the AE and SDR land

Amperos is hiring an Enterprise AE, an SDR and a CSM — and no marketing role. Outbound converts measurably better when prospects can find you on the terms they search after the first touch. Right now they can't, on any of them.

How we checked, and where we'd hedge

Method

13 live Google SERP pulls (US, desktop + mobile) across brand, competitor and category terms · organic and paid-keyword profiles for all four domains via DataForSEO Labs · full-format creative counts from the Google Ads Transparency Center · LinkedIn and Meta ad libraries · direct inspection of amperos.com's tag stack, including the GTM container (GTM-N3TC793C).

Honest hedges

Ad-library databases are samples and undercount — we treat "zero" as "nothing detectable" and triangulated Amperos's Google reading across three independent sources. LinkedIn's library counts creatives over roughly a year, not spend, so 209 means activity, not budget. Meta shows no page for Amperos or Candid; we report that as "no detectable presence", not a verified zero.
