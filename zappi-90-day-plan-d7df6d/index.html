The 90-day conversion coverage plan

Your Senior Manager, Growth Marketing req closes September 17. Even a fast close means a November start. This is how we'd carry that scope until your hire ramps, then hand them a working motion instead of a blank page.

Leaking

Four gated reports are publicly downloadable and Google-indexed — including the exact asset your LinkedIn ads have gated since March, and one stamped confidential.

All TOFU

The 35 newest ads in your LinkedIn library are all top-of-funnel. No remarketing, no demo-led offers, no account-segmented creative. Meta sits at zero active ads.

One gate

Every intent path — any ad, the Forrester TEI page, pricing — lands on the same generic talk-to-us form. No persona, vertical, or named-account routing. No calendar.

Days 0–14

Stop the leaks, set the baseline

  • →Fix HubSpot file-manager visibility on the four exposed PDFs (CPG Mega-Trends 2026, Super Bowl 2026, World Cup 2026, Tariff Survey). Audit the rest of the file CDN, re-gate, redirect the indexed URLs.
  • →Baseline MQL→SQL conversion by source in HubSpot.
  • →Tier the named-account list so everything after this has a definition of "account that matters."
Days 15–45

Fix the gate

  • →Replace the single generic form with routed conversion paths: persona (insights vs. marketing buyer), vertical (CPG / QSR / Finance / Telco), and a named-account fast lane with a calendar on it.
  • →Post-download journeys per asset: Mega-Trends → CPG-specific follow-up, Forrester TEI → ROI conversation. Not one generic nurture.
  • →Wire named-account triggers and routing in HubSpot so ABM plays fire on real intent, not just form-fills.
Days 46–90

Stand up the conversion-stage paid layer

  • →Remarketing sequences: report downloaders → case study → conversion offer. Relaunch Meta retargeting from zero.
  • →Account-segmented creatives for tier-1 named accounts — the "account-based paid budget" the JD assigns to your hire.
  • →Weekly MQL→SQL report by segment, so the November hire walks into a scoreboard, not a rebuild.

The handoff

Documented plays, live dashboards, a ramped motion. Your hire spends their first 90 days improving it, not building it. We work inside your HubSpot, on your named-account framework — nothing to migrate off when they take over.
