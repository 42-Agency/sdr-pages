Where to advertise

  • Greenfield. Most pure-SEO shows (Search with Candour, Breaking B2B, Fractional CMO) run no ads — clean, cheap host-read slots.

  • Verdict: lead with guesting. Sponsor one independent SEO show cheaply to test CAC.
