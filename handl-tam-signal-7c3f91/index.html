How this was built

Same chain as the email you received — shown at account scale.

1

Map the segment

Benefits consultancies & brokerages, plus TPAs and self-funded plan platforms. US only for this sample.

2

Layer signals

M&A, funding, leadership hires, office moves, hiring surges — each dated and sourced, nothing inferred.

3

Check owned data first

Every domain checked against our licensed contact database before buying anything. Most coverage already exists.

4

No fact, no email

Accounts with no verifiable signal get dropped, not templated. Copy is written off the signal, human-edited.

5

Layer channels

LinkedIn from real profiles, segment landing pages, and paid against the same list — when budget returns.
