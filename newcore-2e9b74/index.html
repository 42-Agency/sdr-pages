The finding

You're building the demand. Competitors' pages are collecting it.

Every LinkedIn impression and Google creative NewCore runs exists to make a security leader curious about identity for humans and agents. Curious buyers do one predictable thing next: they search. And on every category and switching-intent result we checked, NewCore doesn't exist — Veza ranks #1 for "non-human identity management," SailPoint and Palo Alto own "machine identity security," and Okta sits on "identity provider for AI agents." NewCore's only page-one rankings are its own name.

Where we'd start

Three moves, in order

01

Buy the category before it has incumbents

The paid slots on "non-human identity management" and "okta alternative" are empty — 350 combined monthly searches of pure switching and category intent with no advertisers captured. For a seed-stage challenger, an uncontested auction on your own category term is the cheapest visibility in the plan.

02

Build the pages the search results reward

NewCore already touches category terms — position 32 for "secrets management," 59 for "identity security posture management" — it just has no pages built to rank. Comparison and alternative pages for Okta, Silverfort and Veza give both the ads a landing spot and the organic results a reason to include you. Veza defends its own alternative result; NewCore currently concedes all of them.

03

Use the audience the pixels already captured

The Meta pixel on newcore.com has been collecting visitors with no Meta campaigns behind it, and the full measurement stack (GA4, HubSpot, PostHog, Apollo) is live. Retargeting warm traffic from 73 LinkedIn ads' worth of clicks is the fastest payback in this plan — the setup cost is already paid.

Receipts

How we checked, and where we'd hedge

Method

12 live Google SERP pulls (US) across brand, competitor-alternative and category terms · organic profiles for all four domains (plus newcore.io, to be sure we had the right entity) via DataForSEO Labs · full-format creative counts from the Google Ads Transparency Center · LinkedIn and Meta ad libraries · direct inspection of newcore.com's tag stack, including the GTM container's contents.

Honest hedges

Ad-library databases are samples and undercount — we treat "zero" as "nothing detectable." Live search pulls often fail to trigger ad serving, so "no advertisers captured" on a term is directional, not proven. Transparency data shows creatives, not keywords, so we can't see what NewCore's own text ads bid on. Labs paid-keyword data returned zero for every domain including Okta this run, so we discarded it entirely.
