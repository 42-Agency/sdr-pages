How ChatGPT describes Steno

Every answer that names Steno says the same three things. This is what the ads will sit next to.

The technology pick

"Technology-first experience" · "modern scheduling platform" · "tech-enabled" — the most repeated descriptor, in nearly every mention.

Flexible payment

DelayPay comes up unprompted and gets called a "distinguishing feature" — ChatGPT treats it as Steno's structural edge for plaintiff firms.

Liked by users

"G2 4.9/5, ~70 reviews, ~97% five-star" quoted directly · "frequently recommended by paralegals and attorneys."

Asked directly: "Is Steno good?" · "Steno vs Veritext" · "Steno reviews"

Positive across the board. "Choose Steno if you value a modern, technology-focused experience, streamlined scheduling, and flexible payment options. Choose Veritext if you need maximum geographic coverage." ChatGPT gives Veritext scale, gives Steno tech, service and payment terms.

Caveats it mentions: occasional billing complaints, Reddit criticism of sales outreach, and a note that some G2 reviews were incentivized. Framed as balance, not warnings.
