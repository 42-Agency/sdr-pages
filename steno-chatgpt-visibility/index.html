When Steno is named, the positioning is remarkably consistent

Across every answer that includes Steno, both engines converge on the same three-part identity. This is the organic message your ads will sit next to — creative that echoes it will feel native; creative that contradicts it will fight it.

IDENTITY 1

The technology pick

"Technology-first experience" · "modern scheduling platform" · "tech-enabled" — the single most repeated descriptor, appearing in nearly every mention on both engines.

IDENTITY 2

Flexible payment

DelayPay / deferred payment is called a "distinguishing feature" and surfaces unprompted in alternatives answers — ChatGPT treats it as Steno's structural differentiator for plaintiff firms.

IDENTITY 3

Loved by users

"G2 4.9/5, ~70 reviews, ~97% five-star" quoted directly · "frequently recommended by paralegals and attorneys" · "strong customer reviews" cited as a reason to choose Steno.

Brand-check prompts ("Is Steno good?", "Steno vs Veritext", "Steno reviews")

Verdict on both engines: positive with minor caveats. "Choose Steno if you value a modern, technology-focused experience, streamlined scheduling, and flexible payment options. Choose Veritext if you need maximum geographic coverage." The head-to-head framing concedes scale to Veritext but gives Steno tech, service and payment terms.

Caveats the engines surface: occasional billing/pricing complaints, Reddit criticism of sales outreach, and a note that some G2 reviews were incentivized. None are disqualifying in the answers — they appear as balance, not warnings.

What this means for the pilot

Each ad-group theme is doing a different job, because the organic baseline underneath it is different.

COMPETITOR KEYWORDS

Ads compound an existing recommendation

When a buyer asks ChatGPT about Veritext or Planet Depos alternatives, the organic answer already says Steno — often first. An ad on the same conversation puts Steno in the answer and below it. This is the pilot's highest-leverage theme: paid + organic double presence at the exact moment of vendor comparison.

GEO KEYWORDS

Ads fill a real absence

In Atlanta, SF, NY, Houston, DFW and San Antonio conversations, Steno isn't in the organic answer at all — local boutiques and Veritext/Lexitas offices are. An ad is Steno's only presence in those green-state conversations. Expect this theme to behave like true awareness spend, not conquest.

DEPO SUMMARY SOFTWARE

Different aisle — creative must bridge

These conversations are about legal-AI tools (CoCounsel, Everlaw, Dodonai), not court reporting agencies. Zero agency brands appear. If the pilot targets this theme, the 50-char headline has to bridge the context ("your court reporting partner also summarizes the depo") or it will read as off-category next to the organic answer.

TARGETING WATCH-OUTS

Two keyword-map quirks

1 · "Accurate Court Reporters" — ChatGPT resolved this to a Canadian (Ontario) firm and answered with Canadian agencies. As a topic hint it may match non-US conversations; deprioritize or pair with US context.

2 · "Stenographer" terms — Steno's literal name-match makes these its strongest organic prompts (first on both engines). Cheap reinforcement, but some of these conversations are court reporters seeking work, not law firms buying.

Methodology

The pilot's Keyword / Targeting Map (Court Reporting, Deposition Summary Software, Competitor themes) was converted into 28 conversational buyer prompts plus 3 brand-check prompts. Each prompt was submitted to live ChatGPT and Perplexity sessions on July 14, 2026 (62 answers total). Every brand named in each answer was extracted and tallied; Steno "presence" = Steno named anywhere in the answer. Brand-check prompts were analyzed qualitatively and excluded from share-of-voice math.
