THE BOTTOM LINE — IN PLAIN ENGLISH

The portfolio's brand strength lives almost entirely in TeleVox's world.

1. In healthcare, TeleVox still has a name.

When you ask AI assistants for patient-engagement vendors, TeleVox comes up about as often as the category leaders. Its 30-year reputation still carries weight.

2. In customer-experience AI, MosaicX is invisible.

In the much bigger conversational-AI market, AI assistants named MosaicX just once in 32 answers. It's lost behind Zendesk, Salesforce, Genesys, Cognigy and the rest.

3. Almost nobody Googles either brand.

Both sit at the bottom of their categories for search demand — TeleVox #10 of 12, MosaicX dead last. People in the market aren't looking either one up by name.

4. Both websites get seen but rarely clicked.

Each ranks for lots of broad, low-intent searches (mostly blog/explainer terms) on page 2 — so they collect "views" but almost no clicks. TeleVox's also has a spam problem inflating its numbers.

5. The April 21 WestCX migration didn't break anything.

Organic traffic held steady through the move for both sites — the redirects worked, brand equity carried over. But the migration also created no new demand, and nobody searches "WestCX" yet.

So what do we do?

Play to the strength: lead with TeleVox's healthcare credibility, and aim MosaicX at regulated-industry / healthcare CX (where WestCX has a right to win) instead of fighting Zendesk and Salesforce head-on. Clean up the website issues, get real category pages off page 2, and keep the trusted brand names front-and-center as "WestCX" earns its own recognition.

02 — WHY THE AI RESULTS DIFFER

Same parent, two very different ponds

When you ask AI assistants to recommend vendors, here's who they name. TeleVox sits near the top of a focused field. MosaicX is fighting mega-vendors (Zendesk, Salesforce, Genesys) and a crowd of funded AI startups — and doesn't make the cut.

Patient engagement — where TeleVox plays

TeleVox is tied for the top of the independent vendors (the EHR giants Epic/Oracle aside). A real, defendable position.

Conversational AI / CX — where MosaicX plays

MosaicX is named once in 32 answers — far below the leaders. The category is bigger, richer, and far harder to be seen in.

03 — WHAT BOTH BRANDS SHARE

Three problems the whole portfolio has in common

NO SEARCH DEMAND

Nobody's looking them up

TeleVox gets ~1,000 brand searches a month; MosaicX ~260. Both are at the bottom of their categories. There's very little existing demand to capture — the brands have to be put in front of buyers, not waited for.

VIEWS, NOT CLICKS

Lots of impressions, almost no clicks

Both sites rank for huge volumes of broad, low-intent searches (mostly blog/explainer terms) — but on page 2, so they're seen and not clicked. TeleVox 0.16%, MosaicX 0.10% click-through. The content earns attention it never converts.

"WESTCX" HAS NO PULL YET

The new name isn't searched

"WestCX Orchestrate" gets essentially zero searches; "WestCX" ~110/mo. AI assistants explain the rebrand correctly when asked — but no demand is flowing to the new name. The equity still lives in "TeleVox" and "MosaicX."

04 — THE APRIL 21 MIGRATION

The move to WestCX didn't cost organic traffic — but it didn't add any either

A site migration is the moment search rankings usually break. We checked weekly organic clicks on both sites straight through the April 21 cut-over. Good news: no cliff. The redirects held and brand equity carried across. The dips you see are mild and recent (TeleVox) or a slow, pre-migration decline (MosaicX, falling since January) — not migration shock.

05 — WHAT TO DO

A portfolio play, not two separate fights

01

Lead with the brand that has a reputation

TeleVox's healthcare recall is the portfolio's strongest asset. Anchor the WestCX story to it — and cross-sell MosaicX's conversational-AI capability into the TeleVox base, where WestCX already has credibility, rather than acquiring CX buyers cold.

02

Give MosaicX a narrower pond to win

MosaicX can't out-shout Zendesk and Salesforce in general CX. Aim it at regulated-industry / healthcare conversational AI — a wedge WestCX is uniquely credible in. Notably, neither WestCX brand showed up for "AI voice for appointment scheduling," a healthcare-CX crossover they should own.

03

Fix the websites: spam out, category pages up

Investigate the TeleVox spam query, then move both sites' real category pages off page 2. They already rank for the right terms ("patient engagement software," "conversational AI") — they're just not high enough to win the click.

04

Get into the lists AI reads, and re-measure quarterly

AI assistants pull from third-party "best software" lists — get both brands onto and up them. Then re-run this scorecard each quarter to watch whether the WestCX name starts earning its own recall. Connect westcx.com Search Console so nothing is missed.

06 — HOW WE MEASURED

Four surfaces, each brand in its own category

For each brand we ran 16 category buyer-questions through ChatGPT and Perplexity (who gets recommended), pulled branded Google search volume and 12-month Trends, and read each brand's own Google Search Console (28 days to Jun 7). TeleVox was measured in the patient-engagement category; MosaicX in conversational-AI / CX. Same method, run 2026-06-08, repeatable quarterly.

Caveats

AI answers vary run-to-run; the pattern (TeleVox present / MosaicX absent) is the signal, not any single decimal. The big EHR and CX incumbents (Epic, Oracle, Zendesk, Salesforce, Genesys) are shown but compete differently than these brands. PolyAI's search figure is excluded as inflated by an unrelated consumer app of the same name. Search Console newly connected — early data may be partial.
