THE BOTTOM LINE — IN PLAIN ENGLISH

WestCX's brand strength is almost all in TeleVox.

1. In healthcare, TeleVox still has a name.

When you ask AI assistants for patient-engagement vendors, TeleVox comes up about as often as the category leaders. Its 30-year reputation still carries weight.

2. In customer-experience AI, MosaicX is invisible.

In the much bigger conversational-AI market, AI assistants named MosaicX just once in 32 answers. It's lost behind Zendesk, Salesforce, Genesys, Cognigy and the rest.

3. Almost nobody Googles either brand.

Both sit at the bottom of their categories for search demand — TeleVox #10 of 12, MosaicX dead last. People in the market aren't looking either one up by name.

4. Both websites get seen but rarely clicked.

Each ranks for lots of broad, low-intent searches (mostly blog/explainer terms) on page 2 — so they collect "views" but almost no clicks. TeleVox's also has a spam problem inflating its numbers.

5. The April 21 WestCX migration didn't break anything.

Organic traffic held steady through the move for both sites — the redirects worked, brand equity carried over. But the migration also created no new demand, and nobody searches "WestCX" yet.

So what do we do?

Lead with TeleVox, the brand people know in healthcare. Sell MosaicX's chatbot tools into healthcare customer service (where WestCX is already trusted) instead of fighting Zendesk and Salesforce in general customer service. Fix the website spam, get the real pages off page 2 of Google, and keep using the known names "TeleVox" and "MosaicX" while "WestCX" becomes known.

02 — WHY THE AI RESULTS DIFFER

Same parent, two very different ponds

When you ask AI assistants to recommend vendors, here's who they name. TeleVox sits near the top of a focused field. MosaicX is fighting mega-vendors (Zendesk, Salesforce, Genesys) and a crowd of funded AI startups — and doesn't make the cut.

Patient engagement — where TeleVox plays

TeleVox is tied for the top of the smaller patient-software companies (ignoring the big medical-records systems Epic and Oracle). A real position worth protecting.

Conversational AI / CX — where MosaicX plays

MosaicX is named once in 32 answers — far below the leaders. Its market is bigger and much harder to stand out in.

03 — WHAT BOTH BRANDS SHARE

Three problems both brands share

NOBODY SEARCHES FOR THEM

No one's looking them up

TeleVox gets about 1,000 searches a month; MosaicX about 260. Both are at the bottom of their categories. There aren't many people already looking — you have to put the brands in front of buyers, not wait for buyers to come find them.

VIEWS, NOT CLICKS

Lots of impressions, almost no clicks

Both sites rank for huge volumes of broad, low-intent searches (mostly blog/explainer terms) — but on page 2, so they're seen and not clicked. TeleVox 0.16%, MosaicX 0.10% click-through. The content earns attention it never converts.

THE NEW NAME ISN'T SEARCHED

Nobody looks up "WestCX"

"WestCX Orchestrate" gets basically zero searches; "WestCX" about 110 a month. AI tools explain the new name correctly when asked — but nobody is switching to it. People still know the brands as "TeleVox" and "MosaicX."

04 — THE APRIL 21 MIGRATION

The move to WestCX didn't cost any Google traffic — and didn't add any

When you move a website, Google rankings usually break. We checked both sites' weekly clicks from Google straight through the April 21 move. No drop. The redirects worked and the traffic carried over. The small dips are recent (TeleVox) or were already happening before the move (MosaicX has been sliding since January) — not caused by the move.

No cliff
TeleVox held ~380 clicks/week and MosaicX ~45/week right through the April 21 migration week.

No new demand
The move was done cleanly, but it hasn't made more people search for the brand or made AI mention it more. "WestCX" still has to be built up.
⚠ ACTION ITEM — TELEVOX WEBSITE

TeleVox's website looks like it may have been hacked

Google shows you every search term that makes your website appear. For televox.com, the #1 term is "pusulabet" — the name of a Turkish online gambling site. It has nothing to do with TeleVox. That one gambling term is responsible for about half of all the times televox.com shows up in Google (~12,000+ times a day), and nobody ever clicks it.

What it probably means
A healthcare site shouldn't show up for a gambling brand. The usual cause is that the site got hacked and has hidden spam pages or links.
Why it matters
There may be a security hole in the site — and the fake views make TeleVox's real Google numbers look worse than they are.
What to tell the web team
"In Search Console, televox.com ranks for 'pusulabet,' a gambling brand, with tons of views and no clicks — check if the site's been hacked or has spam pages."
05 — WHAT TO DO

Treat the two brands as one team

01

Lead with the brand people know

TeleVox is the name buyers recognize in healthcare — it's WestCX's strongest card. Build the WestCX story around it. And sell MosaicX's chatbot tools to the hospitals and clinics that already use TeleVox. They already trust WestCX there, so that's easier than finding brand-new customers.

02

Give MosaicX a fight it can win

MosaicX can't beat Zendesk and Salesforce at general customer service. Point it at healthcare and other regulated industries, where WestCX is already known. One gap to grab: neither brand showed up when we asked AI for "AI voice for appointment scheduling" — a healthcare job they should clearly own.

03

Fix the website spam, then climb up Google

First check the TeleVox site for the gambling-spam hack. Then get both sites' pages higher in Google. They already show up for the right searches ("patient engagement software," "conversational AI") — they're just on page 2, where almost nobody clicks.

04

Get into the "best software" lists, and re-check every few months

When AI tools answer, they pull from "best software" articles and review sites — get both brands listed on those and ranked higher. Then run this same check every few months to see if more people start searching for "WestCX." Also connect westcx.com to Google Search Console so nothing gets missed.

06 — HOW WE MEASURED

Four checks, each brand in its own market

For each brand we asked ChatGPT and Perplexity 16 buyer questions and counted who they recommended, checked how many people Google the brand name (and the trend over the past year), and read each brand's own Google data (last 28 days, to June 7). We checked TeleVox against patient-software companies, and MosaicX against customer-service / chatbot companies. Done June 8, 2026, and easy to repeat every few months.

Caveats

AI answers vary run-to-run; the pattern (TeleVox present / MosaicX absent) is the signal, not any single decimal.
