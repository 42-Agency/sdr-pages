Harbor's market isn't a list. It's a trigger engine.

Every Harbor SKU fires on the same event: an organization becomes active in a new state jurisdiction. That moment shows up in public records — Secretary of State filings, NMLS licenses, Form 990s, hiring, leadership news. Below is a sample of what reading those signals looks like: one account with the signals stacked, and 24 real named organizations, each a live trigger for a Harbor product.

SIGNAL 1 · FINANCIAL
Scale and growth, straight from the 990.

$71.3M in revenue, 99% from public contributions, up 23% year over year. A national donor base that is actively expanding. Source: IRS Form 990, FY23.

SIGNAL 2 · LEADERSHIP
A new fundraising chief, hired to scale donations.

March 2025: appointed a Chief Donor Engagement Officer to lead "regional and national fundraising strategies." New fundraising leadership reliably precedes a wider solicitation footprint. Source: PRWeb, Mar 2025.

SIGNAL 3 · HIRING
A job post naming "market expansion" across 21 states.

Open Business Development role, located in "any of the 21 states where Rise Against Hunger currently operates," focused on "market expansion" and "emerging market segments." Source: public job posting, 2025.

THE PLAY

A fast-growing national nonprofit, operating in 21 states, is expanding its fundraising — yet solicitation registration is required in up to 41. Harbor reaches the Chief Donor Engagement Officer with the registration gap mapped, registered agent and charitable registration ready to file. The engine surfaces this the week the signal appears, not the quarter after.
