01 — THE THESIS

One trigger fires every product Harbor sells.

ZoomInfo can tell you a company exists. It can't tell you the company just crossed a state line — which is the only moment that creates a Harbor need. The map below reads that moment from public records, then routes it to the SKU it triggers.

THE UNIVERSAL TRIGGER
An organization becomes active in a new state jurisdiction.

Expansion, a new license requirement, a new state of solicitation, a new payroll nexus, a spinout, or a lapse in good standing.

DETECTED BY (PUBLIC SIGNALS)
  • • New foreign-qualification filing (Secretary of State)
  • • New state license application (NMLS, state boards)
  • • New-state hiring (job postings)
  • • Out-of-state revenue on a fresh Form 990
  • • New fundraising / development leadership hire
  • • New-office or expansion announcement
ROUTED TO THE SKU
  • Registered Agent — every new state of registration
  • Foreign Qualification — doing business in a new state
  • Charitable Registration — new state of solicitation
  • License & Tax Manager — new license / nexus
  • Reinstatement — lapsed good standing

07 — METHOD & SOURCES

Built on public records. Honest about what's a sample and what's an inference.

Data sources per segment
  • Nonprofits: IRS Business Master File + Form 990 (this sample)
  • Multistate businesses: Secretary of State business registries
  • Financial / mortgage: NMLS Consumer Access
  • Construction: state contractor boards, NASCLA
  • Engineering / architecture: NCEES, state PE/RA boards
  • Cross-cutting: job postings, firmographic-change, expansion news
Caveats we'd want a client to know
  • • The 10 accounts are a real sample, not the full universe.
  • • Each is screened to ≥88% public-contribution revenue, so government-grant, IOLTA, and private-foundation orgs are excluded — these are genuine public solicitors.
  • • Segment counts outside nonprofits are directional sizing estimates.
  • • The "trigger read" is 42's interpretation of public signals, not a confirmed need.
  • • On BOI/CTA: FinCEN exempted US-formed domestic companies from federal reporting in March 2025, so federal BOI is a softer near-term trigger than it was. State-level rules still apply.
