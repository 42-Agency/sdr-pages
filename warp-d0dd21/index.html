The finding

Someone built the tracking. Nobody is flying the plane.

Two Google Ads conversion tags, a Meta pixel, a Reddit pixel and an X pixel are all live on warp.co, collecting audiences and ready to measure. But outside of LinkedIn founder-boosts, no campaigns feed them. Meanwhile the highest-intent demand in the category (people actively pricing your competitors) goes uncontested every month.

Where we'd start

Three moves, in order

01

Conquest the pricing auctions

Point campaigns on competitor-pricing and "vs" terms at warp.co/versus/rippling and /versus/gusto: the pages already carry the proof (multi-state registrations and notices handled, transparent $35 pricing, same-day setup). Not the homepage.

02

Own the brand SERP while it's free

"warp payroll" gets 1,000 searches a month and has no advertisers. Owning it now is cheap insurance against the day Rippling or Gusto decides to conquest you back.

03

Light up the pixels you already have

Meta, Reddit and X pixels have been collecting site audiences with no campaigns behind them. Retargeting warm traffic is the cheapest pipeline in paid: the setup cost is already paid.
