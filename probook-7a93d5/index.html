The scoreboard

Google Search

Zero creatives in the Transparency Center, ever. Zero paid keywords detected, and no Google Ads tag in probook.ai's GTM container. Housecall Pro meanwhile buys "hvac dispatch programs" (320 searches/mo) and "field service management software for hvac" (720/mo) in paid.

Dark

YouTube & Display

No video or image creatives anywhere. Jobber has run 6,000 Google creatives since 2022, text and video, still active this week. Avoca runs an active display program. Probook: none.

Follow ads only

LinkedIn

The Insight Tag is installed and collecting. But the Ad Library holds just 4 Probook ads, all "follow our company" units, no demand or lead-gen creative. Avoca's library holds 48, mostly sponsored video: "Boost Your Booking Rates by 70%."

Dark

Meta

No page, no ads, no pixel: the Ad Library returns nothing for Probook. Housecall Pro has 953 ads live, Jobber 380, Avoca 83 with fresh September creative. The channel where home-service owners actually scroll is fully conceded.

Unclaimed

Brand demand

"avoca ai" gets 2,900 US searches a month at a $40 CPC. "probook ai" returns no measurable Google Ads volume at all. In the AI-for-trades category, the brand buyers search for is currently the competitor's.

Absent

Category SERPs

Probook appears in the top 19 of none of the four category results we pulled: "hvac dispatch software," "plumbing dispatching software," "ai dispatching software," "field service ai." The whole domain ranks for 37 keywords. Housecall Pro ranks for 24,689.

The finding

The closers are being hired. The air cover isn't.

Probook is recruiting SDRs and Enterprise AEs with no marketing roles open and no marketing leader in the org. That means every rep cold-opens into a market where roughly 11,000 measurable high-intent searches a month resolve without Probook on the page, and where the only AI brand a shop owner can actually find is Avoca, live on Google, Meta and LinkedIn simultaneously.

Where we'd start

Three moves, in order

01

Claim the category before someone else does

"hvac dispatch software," "plumbing dispatching software" and "ai dispatcher" are live auctions with no AI-native winner and a wide CPC spread ($19 to $209). The home-services AI dispatch SERP is unowned, and the cheapest time to own a category is before an incumbent decides to.

02

Intercept the 2,900/mo searching for Avoca

Avoca's brand demand dwarfs Probook's. Comparison terms and an honest Probook-vs-Avoca page put Probook in front of buyers at the exact moment they're evaluating an AI front office, the highest-intent traffic in the category.

03

Give the SDRs air cover

The LinkedIn Insight Tag has been collecting site visitors no campaign has ever touched. Retargeting that audience plus a Meta presence in the vertical means the shops your new reps call have seen Probook before the phone rings. Warm beats cold on connect rate, every time.

Method

Honest hedges

Ad-library databases are samples and undercount, so we treat "zero" as "nothing detectable," and Probook's readings were triangulated across three independent sources (Labs, Transparency Center, tag stack) before drawing conclusions. Avoca's Google paid keywords show zero in the sample database even though its Transparency Center is active, so competitor figures are floors, not totals.
