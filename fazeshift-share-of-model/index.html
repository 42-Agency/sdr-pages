01 — TWO METRICS

Share of Model vs Share of Search

Share of Model = how often AI names a brand when a buyer asks it to recommend a vendor. Share of Search = how often buyers Google the brand. Up = recommended more, right = searched more.

The incumbents — HighRadius, Billtrust, Versapay — hold both. Upflow, Gaviti and Tesorio (top-left) are recommended far above their brand size, purely on published content. Fazeshift is bottom-left.

03 — WHAT AI READS

Why: engines recommend whoever's pages they read — and they're reading your competitors'

Before answering, each engine runs a live web search, reads a handful of pages, and assembles its recommendation from whatever those pages say. So whoever wrote the pages writes the shortlist. We logged every page read across the 64 answers: the most-read sites are HighRadius's, Billtrust's and Chaser's own blogs and comparison pages. fazeshift.com was read twice. That — not product, not reputation — is the whole gap.

The pages the engines read most — 64 answers

A STARTUP IS ALREADY RUNNING THE PLAY

lunos.ai — a startup younger than most of this market — is the 4th most-read site in the category, ahead of Versapay's and Upflow's, because it publishes the comparison and "best AR software" pages the engines go looking for. Same play is open to Fazeshift. Unplayed.

THE SLOT IS BEING FILLED

Gemini's top-questions answer has a named slot for "the newest wave of AI-native AR tools" — and fills it with Monk / Paraglide, not Fazeshift. Monk (6 mentions), Paraglide (6), Lunos AI (5) and Tabs (3) all out-score or match Fazeshift (3) in its own cohort.

PROOF THE MATERIAL LANDS WHEN IT'S READ

The one unbranded question where Claude named Fazeshift, it recited the site's proof points unprompted: "8 specialized AI agents; 99% cash application accuracy; SOC 2 Type II; strong ERP integrations." The content converts engines — they just rarely arrive at it.

WHY THIS IS GOOD NEWS

There is no gatekeeper to buy your way past. Gartner was cited in none of the 64 category answers; G2 appeared 4 times. The pages deciding mid-market AR shortlists are vendor blog posts and comparison pages — the price of entry is publishing better ones, not analyst coverage.

AI recommends whoever published, not whoever is biggest.

05 — BRANDED SEARCH

1,000 people a month type "fazeshift". None of them type "fazeshift pricing".

Typing "fazeshift" means someone heard the name. Typing "fazeshift pricing" or "fazeshift reviews" means someone is deciding. Fazeshift gets about 1,000 of the first each month — up from zero a year ago — and none of the second. Evaluation tails run small in this category (HighRadius's is ~130 searches a month), but every established competitor has one. The name is spreading; the buying research isn't visible yet.

MOMENTUM

On Google Trends, "fazeshift" registered its first measurable interest this year, from zero. The incumbents are compounding: HighRadius interest up ~80% and Versapay ~50% over three years.

PUT THE TWO TOGETHER

No one researches Fazeshift on Google, and AI rarely reads its site. So every buyer who knows Fazeshift today was told directly — by a rep, an investor, or a press story. Neither Google nor AI is introducing it to anyone on its own.

07 — METHODOLOGY

Exactly what we did

SHARE OF MODEL

21 prompts × 4 engines = 84 answers

16 category questions (counted) + 5 questions naming Fazeshift (read separately), each put to ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity. Every engine ran a current production model with live web search on, so every answer is sourced and the sources were captured. No counted question names Fazeshift. One pass per engine; all 84 answers returned.

SCORING

Mention tally

A brand scores once per answer however often it is named, so this counts how widely it is recommended, not how much ink it gets. A question's own subject does not score (HighRadius in "HighRadius alternatives", Billtrust in "Billtrust alternatives"). The brand list was built from the answers, not written in advance. Citation URLs are stripped first, so a vendor that only appears in a link does not score. 392 mentions, 36 vendors, 64 answers.

SHARE OF SEARCH

Branded search demand

Branded Google volume (US, 12-month average) across 17 brands × 17 evaluation modifiers. Brands whose names are everyday words (Invoiced, Chaser, Esker) are discounted at the rate their unambiguous equivalents imply; two that could not be cleanly separated are excluded and noted. Fazeshift's own Search Console would replace these estimates with exact numbers.
