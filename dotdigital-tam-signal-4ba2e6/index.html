How this was built

Same chain as the email you received — shown at account scale.

1

Map the segment

Mid-market ecommerce and DTC brands, biased to HQs near your two event cities.

2

Layer signals

Live lifecycle/CRM job ads (some naming the incumbent stack), new CMOs and CEOs, funding, market expansion. Dated and sourced.

3

Check owned data first

Every domain checked against our licensed contact database before buying anything.

4

No fact, no email

Accounts with no verifiable signal get dropped, not templated. Copy is written off the signal, human-edited.

5

Invite, don't pitch

Exec and SME profiles carry the LinkedIn layer; the ask is a seat at Wine Down Wednesday or the Expo dinner, not a demo.
