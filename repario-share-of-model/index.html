WHAT AI ANSWERS WHEN BUYERS ASK CHATGPT + PERPLEXITY · AUG 2026

We asked the answer engines who does eDiscovery. They named 90 companies. Repario wasn't one of them.

18 buyer questions through ChatGPT and Perplexity, 36 answers. Repario appears in none of them. The lists were not short — the engines named eight companies per answer on average, including firms much smaller than Repario.

Ask about Repario by name and both engines answer accurately and well: the Chambers ranking, the project-management bench, the consultative model. The knowledge is in the model. The association isn't.

THE PRINCIPLE THIS READOUT IS BUILT ON

01 — THE FINDING

The model knows Repario and never brings it up

A knowledge gap gets fixed with content and data. A recall gap gets fixed with brand. Repario has the second one.

UNPROMPTED — 0 OF 36
Asked the category question

"Best eDiscovery providers." "Top eDiscovery companies for litigation." "Managed document review providers." Across all 18 questions and both engines, Repario is named zero times.

PROMPTED — ACCURATE AND POSITIVE
Asked about Repario directly

Ask "is Repario a good eDiscovery provider?" and ChatGPT gives a full profile: end-to-end capability, the project-management bench, technology-agnostic delivery, a Chambers ranking, plus the caveat that Repario was assembled from several merged providers. Perplexity names the Chambers band and the constituent companies. Both conclude it's credible.

Nothing in Repario's public brand attaches the company to a question buyers actually ask, so the profile never gets retrieved.

02 — THE ARENA MAP

We grouped the 18 questions into five arenas. Each bar is how often that arena's leading brand shows up in its answers. A long bar means a settled hierarchy and an expensive fight; a short bar means nobody owns it yet. Repario is at zero in all five, so the useful question is which race to enter.

04 — THE OPENING

The arena Repario says it wins is the one arena nobody owns

Repario's own positioning is collaboration — human intelligence plus technology, working alongside the legal team. So we asked the engines that exact question.

"eDiscovery provider that works as an extension of your legal team"

Not Epiq, not Consilio, not Relativity. Five small firms, named because they put that claim on their websites in those words. The engine matched the promise, not the scale.

Repario already believes this about itself. It hasn't said it anywhere the model can find.

Scale is already spoken for

In the plain "who's the best" arena, Epiq appears in every single answer and eleven brands turn up in half or more. Competing here means outspending Epiq, Relativity and Lighthouse on an association they already own.

Managed review is wide open

32 companies were named across six managed-review answers and only one clears half — a legal staffing firm, not an eDiscovery brand. There's no default answer here, and managed review is something Repario is genuinely good at.

The service claim is unclaimed

Across the collaboration and service-model questions, no brand appears in more than 62% of answers and the field runs 29 deep. Buyers ask this question and the category has no agreed answer to it.

05 — THE COMPETITIVE SET

The competitors you brief against aren't the ones the buyer is being shown

Repario named twelve competitors. Four barely register in the answers buyers receive and one never appears at all, while several brands crowding the shortlist weren't on the list.

Relativity is the clearest case. It's a platform rather than a service provider, so it doesn't appear on a services competitor list — but it is named in 61% of all answers, which means it is absorbing the category's attention regardless of how Repario defines its market.

06 — THE BRAND READ

What a brand programme does with this

PICK THE ARENA

Skip "best eDiscovery provider" — that association is bought and paid for. Go after the service-model and managed-review questions, where there's no default answer and the entry price is a point of view rather than a media budget.

SAY ONE THING

"Collaboration" is the right territory and the wrong word — too abstract to get retrieved. The firms that won that arena used a concrete promise a buyer could repeat back. Repario needs its version of that sentence.

MEASURE THE MOVE

This run is the baseline. Re-run the same 18 questions each quarter and brand work gets a number attached to it. Zero to named in a third of answers is something a board can read.

Repario doesn't have an AI visibility problem. It has a positioning problem that AI happens to measure.

07 — METHODOLOGY

Exactly what we did

The full prompt set
Three answers named nobody

Asked for a "boutique provider with high-touch service," ChatGPT returned marketing-slogan suggestions rather than companies. Perplexity answered two questions — managed review providers, and AI document review — with a generic explainer naming no vendors. We kept all three in the count as empty answers rather than deleting them, because an engine that names nobody is a real result: it means the question has no established answer in the category.

Share of Model reads salience among buyers who are actively asking — it observes who the answer engines name, not stated recall in a survey. LLM answers vary between runs; the pattern across a prompt set is stable, the decimal is not. Re-run the identical set to track movement.
