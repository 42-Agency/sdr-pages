03 — THE ENGINE SPLIT

TeleVox does slightly better on Perplexity — the opposite of the AI-native names

The two engines build answers differently. ChatGPT leans on parametric authority, where the buzzy AI-native challengers and the EHR incumbents crowd the top. Perplexity retrieves and cites live third-party listicles (televox.com, ohmd.com, callin.io, selecthub) — where TeleVox, an established 30-year brand, is well-represented. So TeleVox is named slightly more on Perplexity (5/16) than ChatGPT (4/16): its equity lives in the indexed record, not in the model's "hot new vendor" memory.

ChatGPT — TeleVox vs LumaBEHIND

On ChatGPT's parametric answers, Luma (6/16) edges TeleVox (4/16) — the model reaches for the newer "AI access" names first.

Perplexity — TeleVox vs LumaAHEAD

On Perplexity's retrieval answers, TeleVox (5/16) actually leads Luma (3/16) — the citation surface remembers the incumbent.

The lever this exposes: TeleVox's AI presence is retrieval-driven. It rises and falls with the third-party listicles Perplexity cites — a surface you can influence with content, not a fixed brand-memory you can't.

04 — WHERE THE RECALL CONCENTRATES

TeleVox owns its heritage. It disappears at the modern jobs.

Breaking the 16 prompts into buying-journey clusters shows the concentration plainly. TeleVox is present on voice + automated-outreach queries (4/6) — its 30-year core — and on general discovery (3/8). But ask a specific modern question — reduce no-shows, two-way texting, patient recall, deepest Epic/Cerner integration, dental — and TeleVox is named zero times out of 12, even though it does all of them. Each cell = how many prompts in that cluster named TeleVox.

The pattern: AI recommends TeleVox for what it was known for (automated reminder calls, outreach) and for the broad "who are the vendors" question — but not for the high-intent use-case and integration queries where deals are actually scoped. Its recall is anchored to the past, not the platform it sells today.

06 — THE THIRD SURFACE

One read is missing — and it's a quick win

The cleanest possible read of real branded demand is first-party Google Search Console — the queries that actually land on televox.com. It's contamination-free and confirms the search picture from the inside. TeleVox's GSC isn't connected to the reporting stack yet, so this report triangulates the three surfaces we do have. Connecting GSC is the single highest-leverage measurement fix — it would let us see exactly which queries (branded vs category) are winning organic clicks.

08 — WHEN TELEVOX IS NAMED

The identity is clean. The problem is discovery, not reputation.

We asked both engines directly about TeleVox ("is TeleVox good?", "TeleVox vs Artera vs Luma", "what is WestCX Orchestrate?"). When prompted by name, the models describe it accurately and fairly — and, notably, they already understand the WestCX rebrand correctly. There's no identity confusion to fix; the entire gap is getting surfaced in unprompted category queries.

STRONG

Reputation is accurate

Both engines frame TeleVox as a "solid, mature" platform — "one of the largest patient engagement providers," strong in appointment reminders, recall, automated outreach and EHR-connected workflows. ChatGPT produced a fair TeleVox-vs-Artera-vs-Luma comparison table.

THE BRIGHT SPOT

The rebrand already reads correctly

Both engines explain WestCX Orchestrate accurately — "an AI-native orchestration layer… TeleVox is the healthcare brand that uses it." No mangled identity (the failure mode we see in other rebrands). The models did the explaining for you; demand just hasn't followed.

THE HONEST KNOCKS

Consistent stated weaknesses

Both engines cite the same drawbacks: implementation complexity, cost/contract rigidity, and "more focused on communication automation than broader patient experience." Real objections to pre-empt in content — not misperceptions.

09 — WHAT TO DO

Four moves off this data

01

Extend recall from the heritage to the modern jobs

TeleVox is named for voice/outreach but is absent (0/12) on no-shows, two-way texting, recall, Epic/Cerner integration and dental — all things it does. Build use-case and integration proof content so AI surfaces TeleVox at the specific, high-intent moment, not just the legacy one.

02

Own the listicle surface Perplexity retrieves

TeleVox's AI presence is retrieval-driven — it does better on Perplexity than ChatGPT. Get TeleVox into and up the third-party "best patient engagement software" listicles (and keep its own current), and the Perplexity number moves measurably.

03

Sequence the WestCX transition — don't strand the equity

"WestCX Orchestrate" has zero search demand; AI still anchors on "TeleVox." The models already explain the relationship correctly — so lead with TeleVox as the recognised brand and introduce Orchestrate as its engine, rather than shifting to a name no one yet searches.

04

Connect GSC + track Share of Model quarterly

Wire televox.com's Search Console into reporting (the missing third surface), then re-run this Share of Model per engine, per query-cluster each quarter. For a brand with thin branded search, the AI recommendation layer is where TeleVox is found or skipped.

10 — METHODOLOGY

Exactly what we did

Three data pulls, run 2026-06-08, fully repeatable. Built to the Kantar "holistic" standard — full keyword/prompt spectrum, ambiguity correction, product→parent mapping — rather than a naive single-term read.

SHARE OF GEO

20 prompts × 2 engines

16 category prompts (counted toward share) + 4 brand-defensive prompts, each run live on ChatGPT and Perplexity via BrightData LLM scrapers (web_data_chatgpt_ai_insights, web_data_perplexity_ai_insights). Grok skipped (scraper times out).

SCORING

Mention tally

A brand scores when named in an answer. A query's own subject (Solutionreach/Phreesia in "alternatives to…") is excluded from its tally. 172 mentions across 32 category answers; share = mentions ÷ total. Prompts grouped into 5 journey clusters for the heatmap. Brand-defensive answers read qualitatively (Section 08), not tallied.

SHARE OF SEARCH + TRENDS

Holistic, via DataForSEO

Branded Google volume (US, 12-mo avg) on a brand × 14-modifier long-tail matrix; "televox" modeled for the (negligible) Westinghouse-robot collision and reported as a floor→modeled range. Google Trends: 52 weekly points, US, normalized 0–100, for competitor-relative momentum.

THE MISSING SURFACE

First-party organic (Google Search Console) — not yet connected

The ideal third surface is televox.com's own GSC (contamination-free; only counts queries that landed on the domain). TeleVox's GSC isn't yet connected to the reporting stack, so this wave triangulates AI + search-volume + Trends. Connecting it (Rec 04) would replace estimated branded search with the exact query mix winning organic clicks — the single highest-value measurement upgrade for the next wave.

Caveats

Two engines is a directional read, not a census — the engine asymmetry and the cluster concentration are the signal, not any single decimal. LLM answers vary run-to-run; the pattern (heritage-strong / use-case-absent, Perplexity-leaning, high-recall / low-search) is stable, the exact mid-pack ordering is not. EHR portals (Epic, Oracle/Cerner, athenahealth) are included in the tally but flagged — they compete differently. Single snapshot — re-run quarterly.
