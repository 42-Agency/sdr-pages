Where B2B brands actually spend on podcasts

Follow the ad reads and one thing is obvious: B2B brands hardly advertise on B2B podcasts. They buy sports, news, politics, true crime, and tech-culture shows — and reach the buyer as a human, off the clock, at consumer prices.

The thesis

B2B doesn't buy B2B podcasts. It buys attention. The standard unit is a host-read with a vanity URL (brex.com/grow, vanta.com/pivot, indeed.com/podcast) — measurable, per-show, and placed wherever the audience actually is.

The pattern

Reach > endemic

Buy the human, not the job title

B2B buyers listen to sports, news, and true crime — not "The RevOps Podcast." Catch them off-duty at consumer CPMs.

Genre = audience proxy

The show stands in for the buyer

Finance → sports/news. Dev tools → tech-media. Founders → Galloway / 20VC / Masters of Scale. Broad hiring → literally everywhere.

Host-read + code

The measurable unit

A host read plus a per-show vanity URL (vanta.com/pivot) makes a "brand" buy trackable show-by-show. That's why it scales.

The outliers prove it: Vanta, Indeed, and Amex go omnivorous — blanket buys across genres that share nothing but reach. When the product is horizontal (every company hires, every company needs compliance), the "audience" is just "adults with jobs."
