04 — WHERE THE LEAD CONCENTRATES

Artera owns discovery. It fades everywhere else.

Breaking the 16 prompts into buying-journey clusters shows the concentration plainly. Artera is perfect on ChatGPT discovery/shortlist queries (4/4) — and invisible on use-case, technical, and self-scheduling queries, especially on Perplexity. Each cell = how many prompts in that cluster named Artera.

The pattern: AI recommends Artera when a buyer asks the broad question ("best platform for a health system"). When the buyer asks a specific question ("reduce no-shows", "self-scheduling", "deepest Epic integration"), competitors get named instead.

06 — WHEN ARTERA IS NAMED

The problem is discovery, not reputation

We also asked both engines directly about Artera ("is Artera good?", "Artera vs Luma", "Artera vs Solutionreach", "companies like Artera"). When prompted by name, the models describe it accurately and favorably — which means the gap is purely getting surfaced in unprompted category queries.

STRONG

Reputation holds up

Both engines frame Artera as the enterprise/IDN choice, cite KLAS and "1,000+ healthcare organizations", and give it the edge over Luma for omnichannel orchestration. ChatGPT calls it "best overall enterprise."

THE AEO LEVER

Perplexity will cite artera.io

When asked about Artera by name, Perplexity readily pulls artera.io's own blog/resource pages and G2. It can cite Artera — it just doesn't reach for it in category queries. Owning the third-party listicle surface is the fix.

DATA HYGIENE

Legacy "WELL" confusion lingers

ChatGPT sometimes lists "WELL Health" as a separate Artera competitor — not recognizing it as Artera's own former name. The 2022 rebrand still isn't fully resolved in model memory.

07 — WHAT TO DO

Four moves off this data

01

Close the Perplexity gap (AEO)

The #1 GEO spot is one engine away. Get Artera into and up the third-party "best patient communication software" listicles Perplexity retrieves — that's a measurable, ownable surface, not a brand-awareness spend.

02

Win the use-case & switching queries

Artera shows up for "best platform" but not "reduce no-shows", "self-scheduling", or "alternatives to Solutionreach". Build use-case proof and comparison content so AI names Artera at the specific, high-intent moment too.

03

Resolve the WELL→Artera rebrand in-model

Make the former-name relationship explicit across owned and third-party content so engines stop double-listing "WELL Health" as a rival — and so legacy brand equity ("wellapp", ~3,600 searches/mo) routes to Artera.

04

Track Share of Model, not branded search

For a top-down enterprise seller, the AI recommendation layer — not the search bar — is where Artera is found or skipped. Re-run this quarterly, per engine, per query-cluster, to catch movement early.

08 — METHODOLOGY

Exactly what we did

Two data pulls, run 2026-06-05, fully repeatable. Built to the Kantar "holistic" standard — full keyword/prompt spectrum, ambiguity correction, and product→parent mapping — rather than a naive single-term read.

SHARE OF GEO

24 prompts × 2 engines

16 category prompts (counted toward share) + 4 brand-defensive + 4 comparison prompts, each run live on ChatGPT and Perplexity via BrightData LLM scrapers (web_data_chatgpt_ai_insights, web_data_perplexity_ai_insights). Grok attempted; scraper timed out.

SCORING

Mention tally

A brand scores when named in an answer. A query's own subject (Solutionreach/Phreesia in "alternatives to…") is excluded from its tally. 246 mentions across 32 category answers; share = mentions ÷ total. Prompts grouped into 7 journey clusters for the heatmap.

Caveats

Two engines is a directional read, not a census — the engine asymmetry is the signal, not any single decimal. LLM answers vary run-to-run; the pattern (ChatGPT-strong / Perplexity-weak, discovery-strong / use-case-weak) is stable, the exact mid-pack ordering is not. Search disambiguation still slightly understates the most ambiguous competitor names (Klara, Weave). Single snapshot — re-run quarterly.

Why Share of Model matters for Artera

First-touch vendor discovery is moving from Google to AI answer engines. For a top-down enterprise seller with naturally thin branded search, the AI recommendation layer is where Artera is found or skipped — and it's measurable today, per engine and per query-cluster.
