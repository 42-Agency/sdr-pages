03 — CHATGPT vs PERPLEXITY

Artera does great on ChatGPT — and falls behind on Perplexity

The two AI assistants work differently. ChatGPT answers from what it learned during training — industry awards (like the KLAS rankings), analyst reports, and overall reputation. Artera is strong there. Perplexity works more like a search engine: it pulls live "best software" list articles from other websites (such as ohmd.com, televox.com, leadsquared, fitgap, selecthub) and quotes them. Artera rarely appears on those lists. Same company, opposite results.

Artera wins the broad questions — and disappears on the specific ones

But when buyers ask about a specific job — cutting no-shows, self-scheduling, connecting to Epic — Artera barely shows up, especially on Perplexity. 05 — HOW OFTEN PEOPLE SEARCH THE NAME

Counting Google searches for "Artera" — done right

Three different companies are called "Artera": this patient-communication company, a utility contractor (Artera Services), and a cancer-testing company (Artera AI). So you can't just count searches for the word "artera" and assume they all mean this company. If you do, Artera looks tiny — 0.2%, 13th place. When we count only the searches that are really about the patient-communication company (and add back its old name, "wellapp"), its true share is 3.2%–6.2% — 5th or 6th place. Still behind the leaders, but much higher than the plain word-count made it look.

Why Artera's line comes with a warning

You can't fully trust the Trends line for "artera," because the same name belongs to three companies. The bare word "artera" gets about 10× the interest of the clean patient-comms term "artera health," and several times more than "artera ai" (cancer) and "artera services" (utility) — so most of that line isn't the patient-comms company at all. It's the same name-overlap from Section 5, which is exactly why Artera's own Google data (Section 6) is the trustworthy read of its real demand.

08 — WHAT AI SAYS WHEN YOU ASK ABOUT ARTERA DIRECTLY

The problem is getting found, not reputation

We also asked both AI assistants directly about Artera ("is Artera good?", "Artera vs Luma", "Artera vs Solutionreach", "companies like Artera"). When you name Artera, they describe it accurately and positively. So the problem isn't reputation — it's simply that AI doesn't bring Artera up on its own when buyers ask about the category.

STRONG

Reputation holds up

Both AI assistants describe Artera as the choice for large hospital networks, mention its KLAS recognition and "1,000+ healthcare organizations," and rate it ahead of Luma for coordinating messages across phone, text and email. ChatGPT calls it "best overall enterprise."

THE PERPLEXITY FIX

Perplexity will quote artera.io

When you ask Perplexity about Artera by name, it happily quotes artera.io's own blog and resource pages, plus its G2 listing. So Perplexity is willing to cite Artera — it just doesn't reach for it on broad category questions. The fix is getting Artera onto the third-party "best software" lists Perplexity pulls from.

OLD-NAME MIX-UP

The old "WELL" name still causes confusion

ChatGPT sometimes lists "WELL Health" as a separate competitor to Artera — not realizing it was Artera's own previous name. The 2022 name change still hasn't fully caught up in the AI's memory.

09 — WHAT TO DO

Four things to do next

01

Close the Perplexity gap

First place in AI is one assistant away. Get Artera onto — and higher up — the third-party "best patient communication software" lists that Perplexity quotes. That's a specific, trackable target, not a vague brand-awareness spend.

02

Win the specific-need and switching questions

Artera shows up for "best platform" but not for "reduce no-shows," "self-scheduling," or "alternatives to Solutionreach." Create proof and comparison pages for those specific needs, so AI names Artera at the moment a buyer is closest to choosing.

03

Fix the old WELL → Artera name change in AI

Spell out the old-name connection across Artera's site and third-party pages, so AI stops listing "WELL Health" as a rival — and so the leftover interest in the old name ("wellapp," ~3,600 searches/month) points back to Artera.

04

Track AI mentions, not just Google searches

Artera sells big deals to large health systems, so the place that decides whether it gets found is AI recommendations — not the Google search bar. Re-run this report every quarter, by assistant and by question type, to spot changes early.

HOW WE SCORED IT

Counting mentions

A company scores a point each time an answer names it. If a question already names a company (like "alternatives to Solutionreach"), that company is left out of its own tally. 246 mentions across 32 answers; a company's share = its mentions ÷ all mentions. Questions were grouped into 7 buyer-need categories for the grid in Section 4.

Things to keep in mind

We used two AI assistants, so treat this as a clear read on the big picture, not an exact final score. The big patterns are solid: Artera is strong on ChatGPT and weak on Perplexity, and strong on broad questions and weak on specific ones. The exact order of the companies in the middle can shift a little, because AI answers change slightly each time you ask. A couple of competitors share their name with other companies (Klara, Weave), so their counts may be a bit low. And this is a single moment in time — we'd re-run it every few months to track changes.

Why this matters for Artera

Buyers are starting to find vendors through AI assistants instead of Google. For a company like Artera that sells big enterprise deals and naturally has low search volume, AI recommendations are where it gets found or skipped — and that's measurable today, by assistant and by question type.
