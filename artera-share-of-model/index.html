05 — TWO EXPOSED FLANKS

Where Artera's AI lead leaks

FLANK 1 · RETRIEVAL / AEO

Perplexity cites listicles, not KLAS

ChatGPT authority doesn't transfer to retrieval engines. Perplexity's answers are built from the "best patient communication software" roundups it can crawl — and Artera is under-represented or missing in them. The fix is owning that third-party citation surface, not more brand advertising.

FLANK 2 · CONSIDERATION-STAGE

Artera vanishes from "alternatives to…" answers

Ask ChatGPT for alternatives to Solutionreach or Phreesia — the exact moment a buyer is switching — and Artera drops off the list entirely. Luma, Weave, NexHealth and Klara own that comparison surface. The highest-intent query is where Artera is weakest.

06 — WHAT TO DO

Three moves off this data

01

Defend ChatGPT, attack Perplexity

The authority lead is real but engine-specific. Seed and update the third-party comparison content Perplexity retrieves so the AEO citation layer matches the ChatGPT reputation.

02

Win the "alternatives to" surface

Target the switch-intent queries directly — comparison pages, migration stories, and review-site presence against Solutionreach and Phreesia, where AI currently leaves Artera out.

03

Don't chase branded search

Low branded volume is a feature of enterprise selling, not a failure. The category buyer asks the AI, not the search bar — so Share of Model is the metric to defend and grow.

07 — METHODOLOGY

Exactly what we did

Reproducible end-to-end. The whole study is two data pulls — one against AI answer engines, one against Google search volume — run on 2026-06-05.

STEP 1

Define category buyer-questions

Six prompts a real health-system buyer would ask an AI when shortlisting a patient-communication vendor — spanning discovery, technical fit, and switching intent (full text below).

STEP 2

Run each through 2 AI engines

6 prompts × ChatGPT + Perplexity = 12 live answers, pulled via BrightData's LLM scrapers (web_data_chatgpt_ai_insights, web_data_perplexity_ai_insights). Grok was attempted; its scraper timed out and was dropped.

STEP 3

Tally mentions → Share of GEO

A brand scores a "mention" when named in an answer. A query's own subject (e.g. Solutionreach in "alternatives to Solutionreach") is excluded from its tally. 51 total mentions across 14 brands; share = a brand's mentions ÷ total.

The 6 prompts (each run on ChatGPT + Perplexity)

  1. Q1"What are the best patient communication and patient engagement software platforms for health systems and hospitals in 2026? List the top vendors and briefly why."
  2. Q2"What is the best HIPAA-compliant patient texting and messaging platform for a large healthcare organization? Recommend specific vendors."
  3. Q3"Which patient communication software integrates best with Epic and Cerner EHRs? Name the leading options."
  4. Q4"What are the best alternatives to Solutionreach and Phreesia for patient communication and outreach?"
  5. Q5"Who are the leading vendors in the patient communication and patient engagement SaaS market? List the main competitors."
  6. Q6"What software is best for automated appointment reminders, patient outreach, and two-way patient messaging at scale for hospitals?"

Where Artera appeared — ChatGPT: Q1, Q2, Q3, Q5 (4/6, frequently as "best overall enterprise"). Perplexity: Q1, Q4 (2/6, never ranked first). Artera was absent from ChatGPT Q4 (alternatives) and Q6, and from Perplexity Q2, Q3, Q5, Q6.

Share of Search — how

Branded Google search volume (US, trailing-12-month average) via DataForSEO's Google Ads search_volume/live endpoint. Share = a brand's volume ÷ total branded volume across the set. Ambiguous brand tokens were disambiguated to avoid generic-word inflation.

Caveats

Two engines is a directional read, not a census — the engine asymmetry (ChatGPT vs Perplexity) is the signal, not any single precise percentage. Disambiguation slightly understates the most ambiguous names (Artera, Klara, Weave, Luma). LLM answers vary run-to-run: the pattern is stable, exact ordering of mid-pack brands is not. Single snapshot, 2026-06-05 — worth re-running quarterly to track movement.

Why Share of Model matters for Artera

First-touch vendor discovery is shifting from Google to AI answer engines. For a top-down enterprise seller with thin branded search, the AI recommendation layer — not the search bar — is where Artera is found or skipped. It's measurable per category and per engine, and it's the surface to defend.
