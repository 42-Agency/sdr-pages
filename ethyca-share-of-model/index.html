AI knows exactly what Ethyca is. It just never recommends it.

We measured Ethyca in two markets: the established privacy compliance market it sells in today (handling data-subject requests — the "delete my data" and "show me my data" requests companies must answer), and the newer AI data governance market its new product Astralis is betting on. The result is the same in both: AI almost never brings Ethyca up. ChatGPT and Perplexity name Ethyca in just 2 of 12 privacy answers, and in 0 of 14 AI-governance answers — even though, when you ask about Ethyca or Astralis directly, both describe the product (and all five tools) accurately. So Ethyca isn't misunderstood — it's just not getting suggested. And Astralis is launching into a market AI has already filled with other names.

02 — THE OPEN TERRITORY IS ALREADY CLAIMED

When you ask AI for what Astralis does, it names other companies

Astralis launches June 22 around phrases like "AI policy enforcement" and "a data control plane for AI" (a single place to set and enforce the rules for what AI systems can do with company data). But when we type those exact phrases into AI assistants, they hand back a list of companies that already own them — established players moving in from privacy, from data-access control, and from AI risk-and-compliance. This isn't empty territory. It's a market that's forming fast, and it already has names attached.

03 — KNOWN, BUT NEVER SUGGESTED

Spot-on when you ask by name, missing when you don't

The good news: this isn't a reputation problem. When you ask about Ethyca or Astralis by name, both ChatGPT and Perplexity describe them accurately and positively. The facts are already inside the AI — they just don't come up when buyers ask a general question about the market. That makes this a "getting found" problem, which is far easier to fix than the AI getting the facts wrong.

KNOWN ✓

ChatGPT lists all 5 products

Asked about Astralis, ChatGPT correctly described it as the tool that enforces AI data rules as they run, and named all five Ethyca products — Fides, Helios, Janus, Lethe, Astralis — plus its "privacy-as-code" approach. The product details are already learned.

FAVORABLE ✓

"Nimble vs OneTrust's heavy SUV"

Asked "Ethyca vs OneTrust," both ChatGPT and Perplexity describe Ethyca as the developer-friendly choice that builds the rules right into the data — versus OneTrust as a heavier layer on top. Ethyca's pitch lands clearly when you ask.

BUT NEVER BROUGHT UP

Fides is the one thing AI brings up on its own

The only place Ethyca comes up without being asked is the question "open-source privacy tools," where Perplexity names Fides (ChatGPT leaves it out). Fides — Ethyca's free open-source tool — is the proven way in; lean on it to pull Ethyca into the rest of the market.
