The finding

The campaigns are flying. The instruments aren't plugged in.

Most startups build tracking before they spend. Viktor did the opposite: 400 Google creatives live and rotating, with zero Google Ads tags on the site to tell the auction what worked. Google's bidding is optimizing on borrowed signal at best, and the Performance Marketing Lead (Google Ads) and Head of Conversion roles Viktor is recruiting will inherit an account they can't measure until someone wires the tag.

Method

Honest hedges

Ad-library databases are samples and undercount. We treat "zero" as "nothing detectable" and triangulated every zero across independent sources. "No Google Ads tag" means no client-side tag: conversions could still reach Google via GA4 import or API uploads, but neither replaces native tagging for bidding, enhanced conversions or remarketing. Meta returned no data for Viktor, so that channel is marked unverifiable, not dark.
