How to sequence it

A staged, two-track buy — paid reach on one side, founder-led guesting on the other.

1

Test cheap, prove the channel

Open with a host-read on Breaking B2B (no ads today, host pre-sold) and a founder guest spot on The 1% Insight / Best of LinkedIn. Cheap signal on CAC before scaling.

2

Scale reach where competitors lurk

Buy pre + mid-roll on The Marketing Millennials and Exit Five — the exact rooms where creator appetite is proven and a rival already pitched from the audience.

3

Own the category conversation

Book Aneesh onto the endemic shows (Creator Economy Live, Influence Weekly, Stop The Scroll) and layer MATG for awareness. Out-guest the competition before the Marketplace does.
