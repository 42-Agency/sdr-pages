The finding

The channels are on. The proof is anonymous.

Prosper is spending on both of its instrumented channels: the Google Ads tag and LinkedIn Insight tag on getprosper.ai both have live campaigns behind them. But at 9 creatives versus 186, running anonymized case studies against competitors who name TidalHealth, MDCS Dermatology, Concord Orthopaedics and Piedmont Urgent Care, the loudest voice in the category gets to define it. Prosper has 60+ customers and 150,000+ providers on the platform. Almost none of that proof is in market.

Where we'd start

Three moves, in order

01

Put named proof in market

Prosper's numbers beat the competition's (65% of calls automated, $350K saved, 54% booking growth), but they're anonymous while Assort's are named. Converting even five of 60+ customers into named LinkedIn creative closes the credibility gap without a dollar of new budget.

02

Fight on the alternatives SERPs

Vendor-name searches carry 48x the volume of category terms. Prosper's Assort comparison post exists but ranks #13. Paid coverage on alternatives and comparison terms, pointed at proper comparison pages, is the fastest route into competitor deal cycles.

03

Extend Google beyond brand

The Google account is live and converting infrastructure is in place, but with no detectable non-brand coverage, it captures only people who already know Prosper. Assort is already conquesting adjacent software brands. The playbook is proven; it just isn't Prosper running it.
