PART 3 · THE ONE CITATION, IN FULL

The Headless GPX citation, in context

Asked "how can a GP instrument AI agents for fund operations and investor relations?", ChatGPT cited Juniper Square's Headless GPX announcement by name:

CHATGPT · SEP 2, 2026 · CITED SOURCE: "Juniper Square Goes Headless, Opening the Fund Operating System to Any AI" (junipersquare.com)
"Current private-markets platforms are already moving toward exposing fund data and workflows directly to AI agents… rather than forcing AI to operate as a separate layer disconnected from the underlying system of record. Juniper Square"

The answer then recommends the GP build an "LP service agent" on exactly this kind of platform — Juniper Square's product framing, returned as the AI's advice. This is the only idea question where junipersquare.com appears as a cited source. Claude’s answer to the same question did not name Juniper Square.

Method

AI answers

24 questions asked to ChatGPT (chatgpt.com), Claude (Opus, with live web search), and Gemini (with live Google Search grounding) on Sep 2, 2026, from a US context: 6 software-discovery, 4 fund-admin-provider, 4 AI-at-a-GP, 4 private-markets-idea, 2 comparison, 4 direct brand questions. Brand counts cover the first 20 (a question that itself names a brand doesn't count toward that brand). Citation counts cover the 8 idea questions; ChatGPT and Gemini annotate citations in their answers, Claude does not — for Claude we report the sources it consulted while researching. Perplexity was unreachable on the collection day and will be added in the next wave. Single run per assistant — individual answers vary between runs; the pattern (who's present, who's cited) is what's stable and what we track.
