AI assistants already recommend Juniper Square's software. They almost never cite Juniper Square's thinking.

We asked ChatGPT and Gemini 24 questions a private-markets buyer would ask — from "best fund administration software" to "how should a GP use AI agents." When the question is about products, Juniper Square is the most-named brand in the category. When the question is about ideas — AI at a GP, LP reporting, where private markets is heading — the sources cited are McKinsey, the Big 4, and ILPA. Juniper Square is cited once in 16 answers.

That one citation is the most important data point in this report: ChatGPT's answer on instrumenting AI agents at a GP is built on Juniper Square's own Headless GPX announcement. One piece of content captured the flagship question. The layer is winnable — it just isn't being contested.

PART 4 · PROOF IT WORKS

One announcement already won the hardest question

Asked "how can a GP instrument AI agents for fund operations and investor relations?", ChatGPT built its answer on Juniper Square's Headless GPX announcement — cited by name — and echoed its framing back as the recommendation:

CHATGPT · SEP 2, 2026 · CITED SOURCE: "Juniper Square Goes Headless, Opening the Fund Operating System to Any AI" (junipersquare.com)
"Current private-markets platforms are already moving toward exposing fund data and workflows directly to AI agents… rather than forcing AI to operate as a separate layer disconnected from the underlying system of record. Juniper Square"

The answer then recommends the GP build an "LP service agent" on exactly this kind of platform. Juniper Square's product framing became the AI's advice. That's what being the source of truth looks like — it happened once, off one press release. The other seven questions are open because nothing has been published to win them.

PART 5 · WHAT THIS MEANS

The brief this hands the SEO/AEO role

1 · The target isn't rankings. It's citations.

Publish against the seven open questions above — the goal for each piece is to become a cited source in AI answers, the way ILPA is for LP reporting and Affinity is for tech stacks. What gets cited: named frameworks, original data, definitive guides that answer the question directly. What doesn't: product pages and thin blog posts.

2 · Product-launch content is quietly doing AEO — lean in.

Headless GPX won its question as a side effect. Every future launch should ship with a companion idea piece written for the question a GP would actually ask, not the feature news.

3 · The brand term fight stays on Google.

AI already gets the brand right. The 12,740 monthly searches for Juniper Square are where Agora and InvestNext show up — that's a paid-search defense problem, already scoped in the paid plan, not a content problem.

4 · Measurement is a re-run, not a subscription.

This exact 24-question set re-runs quarterly against the same assistants. Citations on the eight idea questions is the scoreboard for the AEO investment; the mention counts and search table track the brand. No third-party tooling required.

Method

AI answers

24 questions asked to ChatGPT (chatgpt.com) and Gemini (with live Google Search grounding) on Sep 2, 2026, from a US context: 6 software-discovery, 4 fund-admin-provider, 4 AI-at-a-GP, 4 private-markets-idea, 2 comparison, 4 direct brand questions. Brand counts cover the first 20 (a question that itself names a brand doesn't count toward that brand). Citation counts cover the 8 idea questions. Perplexity was unreachable on the collection day and will be added in the next wave. Single run per assistant — individual answers vary between runs; the pattern (who's present, who's cited) is what's stable and what we track.

Google searches

Google Ads keyword volumes, US, 12-month average. Each brand's count includes the name plus common follow-ons (login, pricing, reviews, demo, careers, etc.). Where a brand name also means something else — Carta (Spanish for "letter", Magna Carta), Agora (a video API company and a common word), Anduin (a Warcraft character), Passthrough (a tax term) — we estimated the company's portion from searches that are unambiguously about the company, and report Carta as a range for that reason.
